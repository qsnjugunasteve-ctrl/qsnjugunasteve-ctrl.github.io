About Me

I am a Quantity Surveyor with a Diploma in Quantity Surveying (TVET CDACC, Level 6) from NYS Engineering Institute, and an NCA-accredited Site Supervisor for Building Works. I combine measurement and cost skills with hands-on site experience.

During my attachment in the Technical Services Department at Kenya Tea Development Agency (KTDA), I worked on measurement and scope work in PlanSwift, tender analysis and contractor quotations, and the Chai House roof remediation. I was also exposed to KTDA factory projects at Gura, Nyamindi and Mitume.

Most recently, I worked as a site volunteer on the Mary Immaculate Catholic Church perimeter wall and cabro paving project, coordinating a team of 15 workers, tracking materials and monitoring workmanship. I work with PlanSwift, ArchiCAD and Microsoft Excel.

I am looking for a role in site coordination or project management, where I can apply my cost management skills while growing toward a professional QS career.
